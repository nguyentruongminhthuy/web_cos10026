Open positions

SwinEduTech builds digital learning tools and inclusive online education platforms for schools and TAFEs across Australia. We have three roles open in our engineering and design teams. Each one helps our platform work for every learner, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or slow internet.

Working at SwinEduTech

  • Hybrid work with 3 days from home
  • $1,500 yearly learning budget
  • 5 extra days of leave
  • Paid accessibility training

All roles close on 30 November 2026.

Front-End Developer

Reference
FE001
Team
Engineering
Location
Melbourne (Hawthorn), hybrid
Work type
Full time, permanent
Salary
$95,000 to $120,000 a year plus super
Reports to
Lead Front-End Engineer

You will build the student and teacher pages of our learning platform and keep them fast and accessible to WCAG 2.2 AA. In your first three months you will learn our component library, fix accessibility bugs on the quiz pages and ship your first feature with a senior developer.

Key responsibilities

  1. Build page layouts and components with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  2. Test pages with screen readers and keyboard only navigation.
  3. Fix bugs reported by teachers and students.
  4. Review code from other developers.
  5. Turn designer mock-ups into working pages.

Requirements

Essential

  • 2 or more years building websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Good understanding of semantic HTML and WCAG
  • Experience using Git in a team
  • Right to work in Australia

Preferable

  • Experience with React or Vue
  • Worked on a learning management system before

Full-Stack Developer

Reference
FS002
Team
Engineering
Location
Remote within Australia
Work type
Full time, permanent
Salary
$100,000 to $125,000 a year plus super
Reports to
Engineering Manager

You will build features across the whole platform, from the teacher dashboard to the services behind it. Student data is at the centre of this role, so you will help keep it secure and private while the platform grows.

Key responsibilities

  1. Build features end to end with Node.js, PostgreSQL and our front-end components.
  2. Design and maintain the APIs used by our web and mobile apps.
  3. Protect student data with secure coding and careful access control.
  4. Write automated tests and review pull requests.

Requirements

Essential

  • 3 or more years of full-stack web development
  • Experience with Node.js or a similar back-end language, and SQL
  • Working knowledge of the OWASP Top 10 security risks
  • Right to work in Australia

Preferable

  • Knowledge of the Australian Privacy Principles
  • Experience deploying to a cloud platform such as AWS

UI/UX Designer

Reference
UX003
Team
Design
Location
Melbourne (Hawthorn), hybrid
Work type
Part time, 3 days a week
Salary
$90,000 to $115,000 a year pro rata plus super
Reports to
Head of Product Design

You will design new features for our online classroom app. You will talk with teachers and students to find out what they need, then design screens that are clear and inclusive. Your first project is a redesign of the assignment upload screen, which students told us is confusing on phones.

Key responsibilities

  1. Run interviews and user tests with teachers and students.
  2. Create wireframes, prototypes and final designs in Figma.
  3. Check colour contrast and text size in every design.
  4. Keep our design system up to date.

Requirements

Essential

  • A portfolio showing web or app design work
  • Strong skills in Figma
  • Knowledge of accessible design
  • Right to work in Australia

Preferable

  • Background in teaching or education
  • Basic HTML and CSS
Apply for UX003

Inclusive employment

We strongly encourage Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to apply for these roles. If you would like a culturally safe contact person during the hiring process, say so in your application and we will arrange one.

We include this statement because First Nations students are still under-served by many online learning tools. People with lived experience help us build products that respect their cultures and work for their communities.
