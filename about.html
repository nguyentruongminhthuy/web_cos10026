Acknowledgement of Country

SwinEduTech acknowledges the Wurundjeri People of the Kulin Nation, the Traditional Owners of the land where Swinburne’s Melbourne campuses and our Melbourne office stand. We pay our respect to their Elders past, present and emerging, and to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples who use our learning tools.

This acknowledgement appears in the footer of every page. An acknowledgement only means something if it changes what we do, so on this site it also shows up in two practical ways:

  • The job descriptions page invites Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to apply and offers a culturally safe contact person during hiring.
  • Our product goal is learning tools that work for every learner, including First Nations students in regional and remote communities with slow internet.

As Swinburne students we can also point applicants to the Moondani Toombadool Centre, Swinburne’s Aboriginal centre, whose Indigenous Student Service supports Indigenous students on campus, online and in the community.

Aboriginal Acknowledgement: We respectfully acknowledge the Wurundjeri People of the Kulin Nation, the Traditional Custodians of the land on which Swinburne University operates, and pay our respects to Elders past, present, and emerging.
